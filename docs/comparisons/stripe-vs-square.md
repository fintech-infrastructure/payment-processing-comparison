# Stripe vs Square (US Businesses, 2026)

| Criteria | Stripe | Square (Free Plan) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Online card rate (US)** | 2.9% + $0.30 | 3.3% + $0.30 |
| **In-person card rate (US)** | 2.7% + $0.05 | 2.6% + $0.15 |
| **ACH bank debit** | 0.8% (capped at $5.00) | 1.0% ($1.00 min) |
| **Monthly fee** | $0 | $0 (Free plan) |
| **Merchant countries** | ~46 countries | 8 countries |
| **Best for** | Online SaaS, marketplaces, custom checkouts | Retail, restaurants, in-person POS + online |

## Cost Example ($20,000/mo, $50 avg ticket = 400 tx/mo)
- **Stripe (online):** `$700.00/mo` (3.50% effective)
- **Square Free (online):** `$780.00/mo` (3.90% effective)
- **Difference:** Stripe saves **$80.00/mo ($960/yr)** for online card payments on the free tier.

## When to Choose Which
- **Choose Stripe** if your primary revenue is online, SaaS subscriptions, metered billing, or international customers (135+ currencies).
- **Choose Square** if you run a physical store or restaurant and want turnkey POS hardware with unified in-person + online inventory.
