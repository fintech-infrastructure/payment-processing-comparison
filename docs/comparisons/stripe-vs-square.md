# Stripe vs Square (US Businesses, 2026)

| Criteria | Stripe | Square |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pricing model** | flat_rate | flat_rate |
| **Published rate** | 2.9% + $0.30 | 3.3% + $0.30 |
| **$20k/mo ($50 avg ticket)** | $700.00/mo (3.50%) | $780.00/mo (3.90%) |
| **$100k/mo ($100 avg ticket)** | $3,200.00/mo (3.20%) | $3,600.00/mo (3.60%) |
| **ACH bank debit** | 0.8% (capped at $5.00) | 1.0% ($1.00 min) |
| **Merchant countries** | 46 | 8 |
| **Tax responsibility** | Merchant handles tax | Merchant handles tax |

- **Stripe:** Developer-first full-stack payment processor with broad APIs, subscriptions, and global card/wallet support. ([Official pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing))
- **Square:** Omnichannel POS and online payment processor geared toward US retail, restaurants, and small service businesses. ([Official pricing](https://squareup.com/us/en/payments/our-fees))
