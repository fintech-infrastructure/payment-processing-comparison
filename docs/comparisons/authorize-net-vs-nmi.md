# Authorize.net vs NMI (US Businesses, 2026)

| Criteria | Authorize.net | NMI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pricing model** | flat_rate | custom |
| **Published rate** | 2.9% + $0.30 (+ $25/mo) | Custom pricing (Gateway + Acquirer) |
| **$20k/mo ($50 avg ticket)** | $725.00/mo (3.62%) | Custom pricing |
| **$100k/mo ($100 avg ticket)** | $3,225.00/mo (3.23%) | Custom pricing |
| **ACH bank debit** | 0.75% (eCheck) | Custom ACH via acquirer |
| **Merchant countries** | 30 | 30 |
| **Tax responsibility** | Merchant handles tax | Merchant handles tax |

- **Authorize.net:** Established Visa-owned gateway offering both an all-in-one merchant plan and gateway-only bring-your-own-acquirer flexibility. ([Official pricing](https://www.authorize.net/sign-up/pricing.html))
- **NMI:** Flexible gateway-only platform popular with multi-acquirer, ISO, and high-risk US merchants. ([Official pricing](https://www.nmi.com/pricing/))
